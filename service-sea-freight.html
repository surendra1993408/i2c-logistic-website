How it works

From supplier to your door

  1. Day 1–5Goods reach our warehouse

    Your supplier delivers to our China warehouse. We count cartons and send photos.

  2. Day 5–10Loaded and sailed

    Your cartons are consolidated into the next container and the vessel sails.

  3. Day 10–25At sea

    Shanghai or Shenzhen to Nhava Sheva usually takes 14–20 days on the water.

  4. Day 25–35Cleared and delivered

    We clear customs in 2–4 days and deliver to your door.

Questions

Sea freight FAQ

How is LCL freight calculated?

LCL is charged per CBM (cubic metre). For very heavy cargo, every 1,000 kg counts as 1 CBM, and you pay whichever is higher. Use the CBM calculator on our contact page to estimate your volume.

Do I need an IEC to import by sea?

For commercial imports, yes. If you do not have an Import Export Code yet, we can either help you apply or ship under our IEC through our door-to-door service.

Which Indian port will my goods arrive at?

We pick the port nearest to your delivery address: Nhava Sheva or Mundra for the west, Chennai for the south and Kolkata for the east.

What if my cargo is damaged at sea?

All cargo is insured. Report damage with photos within 48 hours of delivery and we file the claim for you.
