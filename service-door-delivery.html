Questions

Delivery FAQ

Which cities do you deliver to?

Anywhere a road transporter can reach in India, including tier 2 and tier 3 towns. Remote areas may take a few extra days.

Can I get goods delivered to an Amazon or Flipkart warehouse?

Yes. We can label cartons to the marketplace's rules and book the delivery appointment for you.

Are there any goods you cannot ship door to door?

We do not ship anything banned or restricted for import into India, such as drones without permits, used electronics, medicines or counterfeit brands. Ask us if you are unsure.
