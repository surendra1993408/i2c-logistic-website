Questions

Warehouse FAQ

What happens if the goods fail inspection?

We hold the shipment and send you photos and a report. You can ask the supplier to replace or repair the goods before anything ships, while your money is still in China.

How do I give my supplier your address?

After you confirm a quote, we send you a warehouse address with your customer code. The supplier writes the code on every carton so we know it is yours.

Can I store goods for longer than 30 days?

Yes, for a small daily charge per CBM after the free period.
