1. Delivery times

Estimated times from the day your goods reach our China warehouse to delivery at your address:

ModeEstimated time
Sea LCL25–35 days
Sea FCL22–30 days
Air cargo7–10 days
Express courier4–7 days

These are estimates, not guarantees. Customs examination, port congestion and public holidays such as Chinese New Year can add time.

2. Tracking

We send updates by WhatsApp when your goods arrive at our warehouse, when they ship, when they arrive in India, when customs releases them and when they are out for delivery.

3. Cancellations

  • Before goods reach our warehouse: cancel at no charge.
  • After goods reach our warehouse, before shipping: cancel and pay only for storage, inspection or any services already done. We can return the goods to your supplier or a Chinese address at your cost.
  • After goods have shipped: the booking cannot be cancelled, because freight has been paid to the carrier.
  • Sourcing requests: the supplier search fee is not refundable once the shortlist has been sent to you.

4. Refunds

  • Approved refunds are paid to your original payment method or bank account within 7–10 working days.
  • If you paid more than the final invoice, for example because the final CBM was lower, we refund the difference after delivery.
  • Customs duty paid to the government can be refunded only if customs approves a refund claim. We will help you file it.

5. Damage and shortage

Check your cartons when they arrive. Record a video while opening them and report any damage or missing cartons within 48 hours, with photos. For insured shipments, we file the insurance claim and pay you once the insurer settles.

6. Supplier disputes

We are not the seller of your goods. If the products themselves are wrong or defective, your claim is with your supplier. If we sourced or inspected the goods for you, we will help you negotiate a replacement or refund from the supplier.

7. Contact

For cancellations and refunds, email or call with your shipment number.
