How it works

You bring three things. We do the rest.

Buy from a Yiwu market stall, a Guangzhou factory or an Alibaba seller. If you can buy it in China, we can bring it in.

You bring these
  • SUP
    A supplierAnyone you are buying from in China
  • P/L
    A packing listCarton count and size, so we know the volume (CBM)
  • INV
    A commercial invoiceWhat you bought and what it cost

Your packing list and invoice are all we need to prepare your quotation.

Then we take over
  1. You get a warehouse address

    Once you confirm the quote, we give you our China warehouse address to share with your supplier.

  2. Your goods ship to it

    Your supplier delivers to the warehouse. We inspect, consolidate and ship, with freight, insurance and customs managed.

  3. Delivered to your door

    We clear customs in India and deliver to your address, start to finish in about a month.
