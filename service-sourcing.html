Questions

Sourcing FAQ

How do you charge for sourcing?

A small fixed fee for the supplier search, plus a commission on the order value if you buy. The fee is adjusted against your commission when you place the order. Ask us for the current rates.

Can you check a supplier I already found?

Yes. Send us the supplier's name or listing link. We check their licence, confirm whether they are a factory or a trader, and tell you what we find.

Why pay through you instead of directly?

We pay only verified suppliers and only against agreed milestones, such as after inspection. You avoid sending money to a stranger's personal account.
