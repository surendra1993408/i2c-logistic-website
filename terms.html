1. Scope

These terms apply to all services provided by ("we", "us"), including sourcing, warehousing, inspection, freight, customs clearance and delivery. By confirming a quotation or sending goods to our warehouse, you ("the customer") accept these terms.

2. Quotations

  • Quotations are valid for 7 days unless they state otherwise.
  • Quotes are based on the volume, weight, value and product description you give us. If the actual cargo differs, we will revise the price and tell you before shipping.
  • Freight rates can change because of fuel surcharges, carrier increases or exchange rates. For door-to-door (DDP) shipments, the confirmed rate is fixed once the goods are received at our warehouse.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Give accurate invoices, packing lists and product descriptions. Under-declaring value or misdescribing goods is an offence under the Customs Act, 1962.
  • Make sure your products meet Indian rules, including BIS, WPC, Legal Metrology and FSSAI where they apply.
  • Make sure your supplier packs goods well enough for international transport.
  • Provide KYC documents when customs asks for them.

4. Prohibited and restricted goods

We do not handle goods that are banned or restricted for import into India without the required licence. This includes weapons, narcotics, counterfeit or branded copies, used electronics, medicines, hazardous chemicals, and wireless devices without approval. Goods found to be prohibited will be held at your cost and may be returned or destroyed. You are responsible for any fines or penalties.

5. Payment

  • Freight and service charges are payable as stated in the quotation, usually before the goods leave China.
  • Customs duty and taxes are payable before clearance, unless included in a DDP rate.
  • Supplier payments made on your behalf are made only after we receive the funds from you.
  • We may hold goods until all amounts due are paid.

6. Transit times

Transit times we give are estimates based on normal conditions. Delays can happen because of vessel or flight schedules, port congestion, customs examination, holidays in China or India, weather or other events outside our control. We are not liable for loss caused by delays, but we will keep you informed.

7. Customs and duty

Customs duty is decided by Indian customs, not by us. Duty estimates in our quotes are based on the information you give and the tariff on that date. If customs assesses a different value or classification, the final duty may differ, except under a DDP rate where we bear the difference unless the goods were misdeclared.

8. Insurance and claims

  • Insured shipments are covered for the declared invoice value plus freight, subject to the insurer's terms.
  • Report damage or shortage in writing, with photos and video of the cartons being opened, within 48 hours of delivery.
  • Damage caused by poor supplier packing, or defects in the goods themselves, is not covered.

9. Limits of liability

For uninsured goods, our total liability for loss or damage is limited to the freight charges paid for that shipment. We are not liable for loss of profit, loss of market or any indirect loss. Our liability to you is never more than the amount you paid us for the service in question.

10. Storage and unclaimed goods

Goods are stored free at our China warehouse for 30 days, then at the published daily rate. Goods that are not paid for or collected within 90 days of arrival in India may be sold or disposed of to recover costs, after written notice to you.
