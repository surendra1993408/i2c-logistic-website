Our story

Who we are

Most of our customers are small and growing businesses: online sellers, retail shops, wholesalers and manufacturers who need parts. They know what they want to buy, but the route from a factory in Guangdong to a shop in India is full of steps they should not have to learn: freight forwarders, bills of lading, HS codes, duty payments and port charges.

We take all of that on. Our team in China receives and checks your goods. Our team in India clears customs and delivers. You deal with one company, on one WhatsApp number, from the first quote to the delivered carton.

Over the years we have shipped electronics, homeware, toys, garments, furniture, machine parts and jewellery for thousands of Indian buyers, from first-time importers to brands that ship containers every month.
